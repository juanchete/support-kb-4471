How the reports split

  1. Parser (379 reports). The shared parser read every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so day and month swapped: “booked for 7 April, the invite says 4 July”. This is the problem #4471 described. Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  2. Form hint (33 reports). The booking-v3 form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants typed what the hint said, and the form read the date the tenant’s way. It looks the same as the parser bug, but only reports sent through the form at booking-v3 tenants have it, and the parser fix does not close it. Won’t fix: booking-v3 is retired.
5 parser reports read as a one-day move: "invite says Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May", "client in Lisbon got Tuesday, client in Toronto got Monday". The desk counts them under the parser, by tenant. A day/month fix does not explain a one-day move, so if they keep coming after 5 October, reopen #3.

Why they were merged

The desk merges on subject alone (decision 0001). Both problems share subjects such as “Date is wrong”, so the rule put them in one ticket. Intake tags do not help either: intake guesses from the browser, and the cause depends on the tenant and the form.

Lessons

  • Count by cause, not by subject. The desk’s own table showed two causes the day the ticket was marked known.
  • Matching on subject merges different problems. A new decision record should replace 0001.
